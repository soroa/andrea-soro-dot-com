App link test

Tap each button, one at a time. Write down what opens: BeeInvolved, the Beekeeper app, Google Play, or nothing.

A. Plain https link

Same kind of link as the email. Tests whether a tap is enough.

B. intent:// with package and Play fallback

Names com.gategroup.beeinvolved. If the app is missing, the browser goes to Google Play.

C. intent:// with package, no fallback

Same as B without the Play fallback. Shows whether the fallback changes the result.
